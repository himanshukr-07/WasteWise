Title: WasteWise Paper Waste Guidance
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/rules-7/

Paper and cardboard should generally be segregated at source and kept reasonably dry and clean for the applicable paper/recycling collection stream. Local authorities or authorised collectors may specify the accepted materials.

WasteWise should not claim that all coated, laminated or contaminated paper is recyclable. When the exact collection route is unknown, advise users to follow local instructions.
