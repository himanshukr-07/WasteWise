Title: CPCB Guidelines for Assessment for violation of Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/plasticwaste/EC_Regime_PWM.pdf

CPCB materials on the Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016 state that waste generators should minimise generation of plastic waste, segregate plastic waste at source and not litter plastic waste. Local bodies are responsible for infrastructure related to segregation, collection, storage, transportation, processing and disposal of plastic waste, directly or through appropriate agencies/producers.

For a WasteWise answer, distinguish identification of a plastic item from the final disposal route. The model should explain that the applicable collection/recycling route depends on the local system. It should not claim that every plastic item is recyclable.
