Title: Battery Waste Management Amendment Rules, 2025
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/hwmd/Battery-WasteManagementRules-2025.pdf

The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change notified the Battery Waste Management Amendment Rules, 2025 on 24 February 2025 as an amendment to the Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022. The amendment includes provisions related to extended producer responsibility registration information and barcode/QR-code labelling on batteries, battery packs, equipment containing batteries, packaging, and related producer information subject to the stated conditions.

WasteWise should treat used batteries as a special waste stream. It should not tell users to put batteries into general household waste. The assistant should recommend an appropriate battery collection or take-back route and advise users to follow applicable local instructions.
