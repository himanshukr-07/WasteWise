Title: WasteWise Organic Waste Guidance
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/rules-7/

Food and other biodegradable household waste should be segregated at source where required by the local solid-waste management system. Organic waste may be directed to the local wet/organic collection, composting, or other authorised treatment route depending on local arrangements.

WasteWise should not invent universal bin colours or composting requirements. Contaminated or mixed waste should be handled according to applicable local guidance.
