Title: WasteWise Metal Waste Guidance
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/rules-7/

Metal items in ordinary solid waste should be segregated at source and handed to the appropriate municipal collection system or authorised waste collector, as applicable. The exact recycling or recovery route depends on the local collection system and the nature of the item.

For small everyday metal or mixed-material objects such as pens, cans or metal household items, WasteWise should avoid claiming a universal bin colour or a guaranteed recycling route. If the item contains electronic components, batteries, chemicals or other special materials, it may belong in a specialised waste stream instead.

When local rules are unknown, recommend source segregation and checking the local authority or authorised collector.
