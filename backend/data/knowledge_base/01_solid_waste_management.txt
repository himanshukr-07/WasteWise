Title: CPCB Salient Features of Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/MSW/Salient_features_SWM_Rules.pdf

The Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) describes the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 as applying broadly to urban local bodies and many domestic, institutional and commercial waste generators. A key duty described by CPCB is that waste generators should segregate waste and store it separately and hand it over to municipal workers or authorised waste pickers/collectors as applicable. Local authority directions should be followed for collection and disposal.

WasteWise should therefore treat source segregation as the default principle, while avoiding unsupported claims that a particular item always belongs in a specific bin color or recycling stream. Local authority instructions can differ.

For household or institutional solid waste questions, the assistant should prefer guidance that explains segregation, separate storage and handover to authorised collection arrangements. The system should not advise open dumping, burning or disposal into drains or water bodies.
