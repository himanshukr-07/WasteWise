Title: WasteWise Glass Waste Guidance
Source URL: https://cpcb.nic.in/rules-7/

Glass containers and other ordinary glass waste should be segregated from other waste where the local collection system provides a separate recyclable or glass stream. Handle broken glass carefully to reduce injury risk and follow local collection instructions.

WasteWise should not invent a universal bin colour or claim that every glass item is accepted for recycling. If the item is contaminated, chemically coated, or part of a special product, local guidance should be checked.
