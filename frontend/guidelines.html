Waste Categories

Initial classification categories used by the AI prototype.

🍌

Organic

Food and biodegradable materials.

📄

Paper

Paper-based materials suitable for the relevant paper stream.

🧴

Plastic

Plastic items requiring the applicable local disposal route.

🍾

Glass

Glass items requiring safe handling and applicable collection.

🥫

Metal

Metal items that may enter the applicable recycling stream.

🔋

E-waste

Electronic items and batteries requiring special handling.

⚠️

Hazardous

Items needing special disposal procedures.

❓

Other / Unknown

Used when the image is unclear or outside supported classes.
