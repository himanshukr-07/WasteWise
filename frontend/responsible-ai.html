RESPONSIBLE AI

Use AI carefully.

WasteWise is designed as an educational decision-support prototype. It explains uncertainty, uses grounded sources where available, and asks users to verify local disposal requirements.

⚖️

Fairness

Waste classification is based on the image and supported categories, not personal characteristics.

🔎

Transparency

The scanner shows the model-estimated confidence label and whether relevant knowledge-base grounding was found.

🛡️

Privacy

Users should avoid uploading unnecessary personal or sensitive information in images.

👤

Human verification

Low-confidence, unknown, special-waste, or weakly grounded results are flagged for review.
